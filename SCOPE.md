# Scope

Scope is stated once, in `frontier.toml`: the question, the inclusions, and the
exclusions. Those bytes are covered by `profile_root` in
`.vela/repository.json`, and `vela status . --json` reports the Frontier name
and that root from them. This file restated the name and the boundaries in
unrooted prose, and the two drifted apart.

External artifacts and agent outputs are source material until an authorized
human Decision accepts an exact transition and replay derives Standing.
